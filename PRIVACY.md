# Pasado CSE Reviewer: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 4, 2026_

Pasado is an independent study app for the Philippine Civil Service Examination (CSE-PPT). It is not affiliated with the Civil Service Commission.

## What Pasado stores
- Your answers, scores, mistakes, study plan, lesson and game progress, settings, and any problem reports you write.
- In the Android app, all of this stays on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.
- If you open Pasado in a browser where you are signed in, your progress can also sync to your account between devices.

## What Pasado does not do
- No ads, analytics, tracking, or advertising IDs.
- No account is required in the Android app.
- Your data is never sold or shared with third parties.

## Permissions (Android)
- **Notifications:** only if you turn on the daily reminder in Settings.
- **Vibration:** for light haptic feedback when you answer.
- **Text-to-speech:** uses your phone’s built-in voice to read questions aloud when you tap the speaker button.

## Backups
A backup code contains your progress so you can move it to another device. Share it only with yourself.
